## B.Sc Nursing (4 years)
**Description (mynursingadmission):** "The 4-year B.Sc Nursing program is a comprehensive undergraduate degree designed to build a strong foundation in medical science, anatomy, and direct patient care. Students undergo rigorous theoretical training and hands-on clinical practice, preparing them to become registered professional nurses capable of working in top hospitals globally."

**Eligibility:**
- 10+2 (Higher Secondary) passed from a recognised board
- Physics, Chemistry, Biology (PCB) and English
- Minimum 50% aggregate in PCB, with relaxation for SC/ST/OBC as per government norms
- Minimum age 17 years on or before 31 December of the admission year
- Admission through the UHSR/DMER Haryana counselling (CET)

**Boards accepted (Shiksha):** CBSE 12th, ISC, HBSE 12th

## Post Basic B.Sc Nursing (2 years)
**Eligibility (mynextexam):** "Candidate must have passed GNM (General Nursing and Midwifery) course after completing 10+2 Schooling (Science stream preferred in 10+2). Candidate should be a registered nurse and midwife with any State Nursing Registration council."

## B.Sc in Paramedical Science (3 years)
- 50 seats, full-time UG degree awarded by Pt. B.D. Sharma University of Health Sciences, Rohtak
- Eligibility: 10+2 with Science stream from a recognised board
- Boards accepted: CBSE 12th, ISC, HBSE 12th
- Specific streams (MLT, OTT, Radiology, etc.) are **not published**, so confirm them with the college.

## ANM (2 years)
**Eligibility (Shiksha):** "Candidate must have passed 10+2 in Arts (Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Biotechnology, Economics, Political Science, History, Geography, Business Studies, Accountancy, Home Science, Sociology, Psychology, and Philosophy) and English Core/English Elective or Science or Health care Science - Vocational stream (only passing out) from recognized Board. Students qualified in 10+2 Arts or Science examination conducted by National Institute of Open School can also apply."
- Age: 17 years on or before 31 December of the year of admission
- Selection: merit based

## GNM (3 years)
Listed on the old official site. The UHSR notice of Aug 2025 shows "Shri Ram School of Nursing Digrota Mahendergarh" in the GNM / MPHW(M) Final exam centre allocation. Seats and eligibility are not published; the standard INC rule is 10+2 in any stream with 40%.
