# Admission Process

## Overview (Shiksha, updated Aug 2024)
> "Shri Ram College of Nursing admissions are based on merit. The institute considers applications of candidates meeting the eligibility criteria. Shri Ram College of Nursing offers courses at UG levels. Some of the courses include B.Sc. and ANM. Candidates can apply online for the preferred course."

## B.Sc Nursing / Post Basic B.Sc Nursing / B.Sc Paramedical
- **Counselling:** Haryana state counselling by **DMER Haryana / Pt. B.D. Sharma UHSR, Rohtak** (portal: uhsrcetadmissions.in)
- **Basis:** Common Entrance Test (CET) conducted by UHSR Rohtak. The old official website said "admissions are made on the basis of the Common Entrance Test (CET-2025) conducted by Pt. B.D. Sharma University of Health Sciences, Rohtak."
- **Quotas:** The college takes part in both Government-counselled and Management quota seats. The 2023-24 UHSR stray-round list shows a B.Sc Nursing allotment under "MANAGEMENT" quota.
- **NRI quota:** Haryana Govt rules allow 15% NRI seats in private colleges (see [../04_Fees](../04_Fees/)).
- **Boards accepted:** CBSE 12th, ISC, HBSE 12th (+1 more)

## ANM / GNM
- Merit based, through the HNRC / Haryana Govt admission process for private nursing schools
- Age: at least 17 years by 31 December of the admission year

## Typical documents (standard for Haryana nursing admission; confirm with the college)
- 10th & 12th marksheets and certificates
- CET scorecard / allotment letter (for B.Sc courses)
- Haryana domicile certificate (for state quota)
- Caste / category certificate (if applicable)
- Aadhaar card, passport-size photographs
- Character certificate, migration certificate (if board is outside Haryana)
- Medical fitness certificate
- GNM certificate & nursing registration (for Post Basic B.Sc Nursing)
