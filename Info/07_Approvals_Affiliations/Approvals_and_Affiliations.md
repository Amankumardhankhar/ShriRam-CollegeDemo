# Approvals & Affiliations

| Body | Role | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **Indian Nursing Council (INC), New Delhi** | National regulator for nursing courses; approves B.Sc, PB B.Sc, GNM, ANM | Old official site; mynursingadmission; Shiksha ("approved by eminent Government bodies such as INC") |
| **Haryana Nurses & Midwives Registration Council (HNRC)** | State council; recognises the institution and registers nurses; runs ANM/GNM | Old official site; mynursingadmission; nursinginindia.com |
| **Pt. B.D. Sharma University of Health Sciences (UHSR), Rohtak** | Affiliating university for the degrees (B.Sc Nursing, PB B.Sc, B.Sc Paramedical) and examining body for GNM/MPHW | Old official site; Shiksha (paramedical "Degree by Pandit Bhagwat Dayal Sharma University of Health Sciences"); UHSR notices |
| **Govt. of Haryana / DMER Haryana** | Recognition; runs admission counselling; fixes fees | nursinginindia.com ("recognized by the Government of Haryana"); DMER/UHSR 2023-24 counselling list; Haryana Gazette fee notification 2024 |

   URL: http://uhsr.ac.in/writereaddata/upload/Admissions/221123listeligible.pdf (now offline; archived on the Wayback Machine)
