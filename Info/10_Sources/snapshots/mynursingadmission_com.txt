About Shri Ram College Of Nursing - Mahendragarh 
Shri ram College of Nursing was established in the year 2019. The College is located at Mahendragarh District in Haryana. The College is approved by INC and Haryana Nurses and Midwives Council.
Nursing institute has spacious lecture halls with comfortable seating arrangements and equipped with advanced teaching aids. The institute has a full-fledged library with latest medical & nursing books, Indian and International e-journals, newspapers, periodicals and magazines. The lab is equipped with models, charts, specimens, microscopes and slides to provide better understanding of Human Anatomy. Physiology and Microbiology. The audiovisual lab consist of OHP, LCD, TV, Posters, Flannel board, Model and other teaching learning materials for effective teaching learning process. Through collaborative efforts, the college ensures that students not only gain knowledge but also grow in confidence and capability.
