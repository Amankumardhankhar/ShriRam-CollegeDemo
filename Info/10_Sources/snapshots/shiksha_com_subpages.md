## Shri Ram College of Nursing Admission Process 2026
Shri Ram College of Nursing admissions are based on merit. The institute considers applications of candidates meeting the eligibility criteria. [Shri Ram College of Nursing](https://www.shiksha.com/college/shri-ram-college-of-nursing-mahendragarh-195177) offers courses at UG levels. Some of the courses include B.Sc. and ANM. Candidates can apply online for the preferred course.
The institute offers UG courses for 2 years - 3 years.
