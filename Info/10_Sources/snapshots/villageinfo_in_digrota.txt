Digrota is a village located in Satnali St tehsil of Mahendragarh district, Haryana, India. It is approximately 25 km from Mahendragarh. Digrota is also a designated Gram Panchayat, headed by an elected Sarpanch. 
For political representation, the village falls under the Mahendragarh Vidhan Sabha (Assembly) constituency and the Bhiwani-Mahendragarh Lok Sabha (Parliamentary) constituency.
The LGD village code for Digrota is 61976. It covers a total geographical area of 1542 hectares (15.42 km²) and falls under the 123024 pincode. Mahendergarh is the nearest town.

Population of Digrota
According to the 2011 Census, Digrota village had a total population of 3,310 people, including 1,721 males and 1,589 females, living in 669 households. The sex ratio was 923 females per 1,000 males. The overall literacy rate was 72.13%, with male literacy at 84.39% and female literacy at 58.81%. The Scheduled Caste (SC) population was 977.
