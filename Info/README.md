## ⚠️ Read this first

> **Update (Google Maps):** the college's Google listing is named exactly **"Shri Ram college of Medical Science & Research"**. Its own posts also show the managing society (**Shri Ram Krishan Param Hans Shiksha Parishad**), the **SRGI** brand, a **BAMS** (Ayurveda) college on the same campus, the leadership (2018), and phone **01285-222514**. See [01_Overview/Google_Maps_Findings.md](01_Overview/Google_Maps_Findings.md).

1. **Name on the internet.** The Google Maps listing and the college's own banners use **"Shri Ram College of Medical Science & Research"** (confirmed). The education directories list the nursing units separately as:
   - **Shri Ram College of Nursing**, Digrota, Mahendragarh. It runs the degree courses: B.Sc Nursing, Post Basic B.Sc Nursing and B.Sc Paramedical.
   - **Shri Ram School of Nursing**, Digrota, Mahendragarh. It runs the diploma courses (ANM/GNM), and UHSR's official documents use this name.
   - The old official website was **srgroupofcolleges.com** ("SR Group of Colleges").

   "Shri Ram College of Medical Science & Research" is the umbrella name, run by Shri Ram Krishan Param Hans Shiksha Parishad under the SRGI brand, and it also covers an Ayurvedic (BAMS) college.
2. **The official website is offline.** `srgroupofcolleges.com` no longer resolves because the domain has expired. The Wayback Machine has no archived copy either, so the college's own text could not be recovered. What survives of it is in the search-engine snippets quoted in the files below.
3. **Sources disagree on some facts**, such as the year of establishment and the seat count. Every conflict is listed in [01_Overview/Data_Conflicts_and_Gaps.md](01_Overview/Data_Conflicts_and_Gaps.md). Get the college to confirm these before they go on the website.
