Admissions to Shri Ram College of Nursing and Shri Ram School of Nursing follow the rules of the Indian Nursing Council, the Department of Medical Education & Research (DMER), Haryana, and Pt. B.D. Sharma University of Health Sciences, Rohtak.

Admission process

B.Sc Nursing, Post Basic B.Sc & B.Sc Paramedical

  1. Register for the entrance test / counselling on the UHSR admissions portal (uhsrcetadmissions.in) when it is notified.
  2. Appear in the entrance test (CET) conducted by Pt. B.D. Sharma UHS, Rohtak.
  3. Fill in choices during online counselling and select Shri Ram College of Nursing, Digrota, Mahendergarh.
  4. On allotment, report to the college with original documents and pay the fee within the given dates.

Management-quota seats are filled as per state rules. Contact the college office for details.

GNM & ANM

  1. Obtain the application form from the college office (or enquire online).
  2. Admission is made on merit in the qualifying examination, as per HNRC / Govt. of Haryana norms.
  3. Attend document verification.
  4. Pay the fee and complete enrolment.

Eligibility at a glance

Documents required

  • 10th and 12th mark sheets and certificates (originals + 2 self-attested copies)
  • Entrance test score card and allotment letter (B.Sc courses)
  • Haryana domicile / resident certificate (for state-quota seats)
  • Caste / category certificate, if applicable
  • Aadhaar card and 6 recent passport-size photographs
  • Character certificate from the last institution attended
  • Migration certificate (for boards outside Haryana)
  • Medical fitness certificate
  • GNM certificate and nursing council registration (Post Basic B.Sc only)

Frequently asked questions

Is the college approved by the Indian Nursing Council?

Yes. Our nursing programmes are approved by the Indian Nursing Council (INC), New Delhi, and the Haryana Nurses & Midwives Council. See Approvals & Affiliations.

Which university awards the degree?

Degrees are awarded by Pt. B.D. Sharma University of Health Sciences (UHSR), Rohtak.

Is hostel accommodation available?

Yes. There are separate hostels for girls and boys, with mess facilities and security.

Is the college co-educational?

Yes. Both male and female candidates can apply, subject to the eligibility rules of each course.

Where do students do their clinical training?

At our own 100-bed hospital, plus community health postings. See Hospital.

What are the fees?

Fees follow the structure notified by the Government of Haryana. See Fee Structure.
