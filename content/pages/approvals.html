Our programmes are approved by the national and state regulators for nursing education, and our degrees are awarded by the state health sciences university.

National regulator

Indian Nursing Council, New Delhi

Approves the B.Sc Nursing, Post Basic B.Sc Nursing, GNM and ANM programmes and their intake.

State council

Haryana Nurses & Midwives Council

Recognises the institution in Haryana and registers our graduates as nurses and midwives.

Affiliating university

Pt. B.D. Sharma University of Health Sciences, Rohtak

Awards our degrees, conducts entrance tests, counselling and examinations (including GNM finals).
