Our campus at Digrota is designed for focused study, practical training and a safe residential life.

Classrooms

Spacious, fully furnished lecture halls with comfortable seating and advanced teaching aids.

Laboratories

Labs with models, charts, specimens, microscopes and slides for anatomy, physiology and microbiology, plus nursing skill labs for practical training.

Audio-visual lab

OHP, LCD projector, TV, posters, flannel boards, models and other teaching-learning material.

Library

A full library with 3,500+ medical and nursing books, 10 journals, Indian and international e-journals, newspapers and magazines.

Auditorium

A venue for seminars, workshops, guest lectures and cultural events.

Hostels

Separate hostels for girls and boys with mess facilities and security.

Canteen

A hygienic canteen serving meals and refreshments on campus.

Transport

College buses connect the campus with nearby towns and villages.

Wi-Fi campus

Internet access for learning and research across the campus.

Gym & sports

A gym and sports complex for fitness and recreation.

Hospital

Our own 100-bed hospital for clinical training.

Medical care

Medical facilities for students on campus.
