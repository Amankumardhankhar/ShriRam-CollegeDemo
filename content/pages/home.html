About the college

Quality nursing education in the heart of rural Haryana

Shri Ram College of Medical Science & Research at Digrota, Mahendragarh, runs Shri Ram College of Nursing and Shri Ram School of Nursing, offering degree and diploma programmes in nursing and allied health.

Own 100-bed hospitalClinical exposure from the early semesters.

Modern laboratoriesAnatomy, physiology, microbiology & nursing skills.

Well-stocked library3,500+ books, journals and e-resources.

Safe hostelsSeparate hostels for girls and boys, with mess.

College transportBuses on routes around the campus.

Life at Shri Ram

A campus built for learning and care

Our pillars

Care. Compassion. Competence.

Three values that guide how we teach and how our students serve.

Care

Patient-centred nursing is at the heart of everything we teach, from the first classroom lesson to the hospital ward.

Compassion

We shape professionals who treat every patient with dignity, empathy and respect, whatever their background.

Competence

Strong science, supervised clinical practice and professional ethics prepare graduates for real-world healthcare.
