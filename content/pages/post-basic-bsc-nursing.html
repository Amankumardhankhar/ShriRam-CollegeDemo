A two-year degree for registered GNM nurses who want to upgrade to a B.Sc qualification and take on greater clinical and leadership roles.

Key facts

Eligibility

  • Passed 10+2 (Science stream preferred)
  • Passed the GNM (General Nursing & Midwifery) course
  • Registered nurse and midwife with a State Nursing Registration Council

What you will study

  • Advanced nursing practice and nursing education
  • Community health nursing
  • Maternal, child and mental health nursing
  • Introduction to nursing research and statistics
  • Nursing administration and ward management

Career opportunities

  • Senior staff nurse and ward in-charge roles
  • Public health and community nursing
  • Nursing tutor / clinical instructor
  • Higher studies: M.Sc Nursing
